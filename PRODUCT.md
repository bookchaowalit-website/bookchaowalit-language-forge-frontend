# Language Forge

Language Forge is a focused language practice desk for turning a phrase into usable memory. The first surface should let a learner choose a small word set, inspect meaning and pronunciation, answer a short prompt, and see session progress without pretending to be a full course or live translation service.

## Audience and scene

A curious independent learner using a laptop or phone for a deliberate 8-minute practice session. Content is illustrative and synthetic; no account, streak, or live language data is claimed.

## Success

The learner can pick a phrase, answer one prompt, get clear feedback, and understand what remains in the session within seconds.

## Boundaries

- Keep the product self-contained and client-side.
- Use a small set of synthetic Thai/English phrase records.
- Make answer, next phrase, and reset controls work.
- Avoid generic education-dashboard patterns and marketing claims.
